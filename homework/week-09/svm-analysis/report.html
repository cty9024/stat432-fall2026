CHD classification with support vector machines

SAheart data | 462 observations | 9 predictors

Selected: linear SVM, C=0.1. CV error 28.8% ± 1.4% SE. It is the simplest model within one SE of the minimum CV error.

Data and model selection

There are 302 non-cases and 160 CHD cases (34.6%); no missing values; 83 observations have at least one 1.5-IQR predictor outlier. SVM comparison is reasonable for this moderate-sized binary task. Famhist was coded Absent=1, Present=2; scaling was fit within training folds. A stratified 25% test set (seed 432) was reserved before tuning; training used 5-fold CV repeated 3 times. The 67 candidates included linear, RBF, and degree-2/3 polynomial kernels (polynomial coef0=1); C={0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100}; gamma={0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1}, plus two quadratic boundary probes: (C=0.001, gamma=1) and (C=0.01, gamma=10).

The selected fit uses 216 support vectors (62.4% of training cases) and has a linear boundary in standardized predictor space. RBF had the lowest CV error (27.2% ± 1.7% SE), but its advantage over the selected linear model was not clear by the one-SE rule. This is predictive, not causal; individual predictor effects are not claimed.
