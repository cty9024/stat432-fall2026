# Explain to Me

Help me understand a homework question before I try to solve it myself.

When I explicitly ask you to use this skill:

1. Explain the main goal of the question in simple language.
2. Break the question into the specific tasks I am expected to complete.
3. Explain the statistical concepts or notation that I need to understand.
4. When relevant, point me to the appropriate Week 3 lecture material, especially:
   - Ridge Regression: Stability Through Shrinkage
   - From a Penalized Objective to a Fitted Ridge Model
5. Give hints or guiding questions that help me start the problem.

Do not complete the homework solution, perform the full derivation, or give the final answer for me.
